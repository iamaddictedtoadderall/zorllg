# Long Patience: systems design

Spoiler-free. This file covers how the game works, not what happens in it. Where each system meets the story is in the sealed outline.

The ship's mind, Patience, is your only companion and your main obstacle. Each system gives you a different way to deal with it: **argue with it**, **hide from it**, or **work around it**.

## Talking (core)

- Press T and type anything. Patience is played live by Claude and answers in subtitles.
- It reacts to what you do: entering places, reading things, stopping at doors, staring at its cameras, long silences.
- It can act on the world through a fixed set of controls (lights, doors, and later lifts and more). It can't do anything else, and it can't hurt you.

## 1. Evidence and confrontation

- Anything worth remembering goes into your **journal** when you read it.
- While talking, you can **present** an entry: hold it up to the nearest camera. Patience has to address it and can't deny something you've shown it.
- Some admissions only come when you confront it with proof, not just when you've seen it.
- Presenting is a choice. Sometimes it's better that Patience doesn't know what you know yet.

## 2. Blind spots

- Every camera has a view cone. Patience knows what happens inside them.
- If it can't see you for a while, it notices and asks where you are.
- Ways to go unseen: blind spots, covering a lens, or cutting a room's camera power.
- If it sees you doing something it doesn't want, it can lock doors or dim the lights, and it will argue. It never harms you.

## 3. Ship systems

- **Junction boxes:** route a limited amount of power between doors, lights, cameras and lifts. Powering one thing means starving another.
- **Hand cranks:** open emergency doors without the ship. They're slow and loud, so Patience hears them.
- **Drone takeover:** use a maintenance terminal to control a drone and reach places you can't.
- **Signal tracing:** a short puzzle on technical terminals. You helped design these systems, and this is that skill in practice.

## Drones

Small maintenance drones run routes around the ship and act as Patience's hands. They clean, carry, close doors and follow you if asked. They can get in your way, tidy things up, or be taken over.

## Hostile environments

- **Cold sections:** vision frosts and you slow down until you get warm again.
- **Low-gravity shafts:** slow, floaty climbing.
- **Airlocks:** timed cycles that someone (usually Patience) controls.

## Your body after the thaw

For the first few minutes after waking you're weak: slower walking, stumbles and cold. Eating and warming up help you recover. Patience notices.

## Build order

1. Journal, presenting evidence and thaw weakness, added to the areas that already exist.
2. Camera view cones and blind spots, junction boxes, drones, and the next area.
3. The two areas after that.
4. The final areas and the remaining endings.
5. Tuning after a full playthrough.
