# Open to-dos (player feedback)

## Clarity pass: terms and radio messages (from the player, after the rough L1 test)
The player found the made-up terms and radio messages confusing: the script refers to things before the game introduces them. Fix before release, without spoilers:
- Plain weapon names on the HUD (Rifle, Blade, Missiles, Repair kits); the flavour name small underneath or dropped.
- First-time explainer: the first time any term or mechanic appears (TEAR, the rack, the Bench, sledges, water, etc.), show a short plain-English hint with the key to press.
- Speaker tags: the first time each radio voice speaks, a one-line tag says who they are to the player (no spoilers).
- Rewrite level 1 comms lines that use a term before it is introduced (introduce it earlier or say it plainly).
- "Field notes" page in the pause menu listing every term met so far.
